# Privacy

Panna does not collect, send or share any data.

- No accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
- Books, highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading positions and settings are stored only inside the app on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.
- The home-screen widget, if you add one, shows the current book's title, cover and progress on your home screen. Its copy of the cover is kept inside the app and deleted when the book leaves your recent list.
- App data is excluded from Google's cloud backup. Android's phone-to-phone transfer can copy it to a new phone you set up.
- Files leave the app only when you choose to share them (a book, your notes, or a backup), through Android's share sheet.
- Read aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. Some engines may use the network for certain voices; that is controlled by the engine you have installed, not by Panna.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
