# Privacy

Panna does not collect, send or share any data.

- No accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
- Books, highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading positions and settings are stored only inside the app on your phone. They are left out of Google's cloud backup; moving to a new phone with a cable or Wi-Fi transfer copies them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.
- Book content cannot reach the internet: the app's content security policy blocks any address outside the app, so an EPUB cannot load trackers or run scripts.
- Files leave the app only when you choose to share them (a book, your notes, or a backup), through Android's share sheet.
- Read aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. Some engines may use the network for certain voices; that is controlled by the engine you have installed, not by Panna.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
