# Privacy

Panna does not collect, send or share any data.

- No accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
- Books, highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading positions and settings are stored only inside the app on your device. On a phone or tablet, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them. On a computer they are in Panna's folder in your user profile (`%APPDATA%\Panna` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/Panna` on macOS, `~/.config/Panna` on Linux), which uninstalling may leave behind.
- The home-screen widget, if you add one, shows the current book's title, cover and progress on your home screen. Its copy of the cover is kept inside the app and deleted when the book leaves your recent list.
- On Android, app data is excluded from Google's cloud backup; Android's phone-to-phone transfer can copy it to a new phone you set up. On iPhone and iPad, like other apps' data, it is part of your device backups (iCloud or a computer) if you make them.
- Panna goes online for one thing only: if you tap **Download Wiktionary** under Settings → Dictionary, it downloads that dictionary file from this project's GitHub releases (github.com). Like any download, GitHub sees your IP address; Panna sends nothing else and the words you look up never leave the device. Without that tap, Panna makes no network connections at all, and it never checks for updates (new versions are on the Releases page).
- Files leave the app only when you choose to share or save them (a book, your notes, or a backup), through the system's share sheet or save dialog.
- Read aloud uses your device's own text-to-speech voices. Some engines may use the network for certain voices; that is controlled by the engine you have installed, not by Panna.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
